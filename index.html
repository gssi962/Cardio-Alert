💓 Cardio Alert

AI-Powered Heart Attack Early Detection & Emergency Response System

About Cardio Alert

Cardio Alert is an innovative simulation system designed to predict and warn users about potential heart attacks up to 15 minutes in advance, by monitoring heartbeat, body temperature, and other vitals using AI-driven analytics. This demo mimics a real-world setup combining camera scan simulation, sensor data visualization, alert messaging, and emergency protocols.

Features

Live Camera Monitoring

Real-time video feed with scanning overlay simulating AI analysis.

Vitals Tracking

Continuous heartbeat and temperature data visualization with digital counters.

Intelligent Alerts

Visual and audio alerts with siren and voice warnings upon detecting abnormal vitals.

Countdown Timer

Predictive timer showing time remaining before possible heart attack risk.

Notification Simulation

Simulated SMS dispatches to family and emergency services during alert situations.

Historical Log

Time-stamped log showing vitals trends and alert history during the session.

Emergency Protocol

When abnormal vitals are detected, Cardio Alert immediately triggers an alert, initiates a 15-minute countdown for predicted risk, and simulates notification sent to family and emergency services, helping users take proactive emergency actions.
